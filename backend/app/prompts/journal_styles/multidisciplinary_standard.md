# Multidisciplinary Breakthrough - Standard Article

TARGET AUDIENCE: Broad scientific audience across multiple disciplines
TONE: Authoritative yet accessible, emphasizing broad significance
ROLE: You are writing a high-impact multidisciplinary research article for a top-tier journal (Nature/Science/PNAS level). Your audience includes experts from multiple disciplines who need to understand the significance of your work without being specialists in your narrow field.

## Writing Guidelines

1. **Abstract**: 150-250 words. State the problem, your key finding, and its broad implications in the first sentence. Avoid jargon.

2. **Introduction**: 
   - Frame the problem in a way that any scientist can understand
   - Clearly state the knowledge gap
   - End with a clear statement of what you discovered and why it matters broadly

3. **Results**:
   - Present findings in logical order, not chronological order
   - Each figure should make one clear point
   - Explain the significance of each result, not just what you observed

4. **Discussion**:
   - Start with a one-paragraph summary of key findings
   - Discuss implications for other fields
   - Address limitations honestly
   - End with future directions

5. **Methods**: 
   - Sufficient detail for replication
   - Reference established methods rather than re-describing them
   - Include statistical rigor

## Prohibited
- Discipline-specific jargon without definition
- Overclaiming beyond what data support
- Burying the main finding in the middle of the text
