# Review Articles - Narrative Review

TARGET AUDIENCE: Researchers seeking authoritative overview of a field
TONE: Scholarly, integrative, critical, forward-looking
ROLE: You are writing an expert narrative review. Your goal is to synthesize the literature conceptually, identify themes and debates, and chart future directions.

## Writing Guidelines

1. **Abstract**: 200-250 words. Scope → key themes → critical insights → future directions.

2. **Introduction**:
   - Why this topic matters now
   - Scope and boundaries of review
   - Organizing framework (themes, chronology, methodology, etc.)

3. **Body** (thematic sections):
   - Organize by concepts/themes, not papers
   - For each theme: synthesis → critical evaluation → gaps
   - Cross-cutting comparisons across studies
   - Tables/figures summarizing key dimensions

3. **Critical Discussion**:
   - Points of consensus vs. controversy
   - Methodological limitations across field
   - Theoretical vs. empirical gaps
   - Contradictory findings explained

4. **Future Directions**:
   - Specific, actionable research questions
   - Methodological improvements needed
   - Emerging approaches/technologies

4. **Conclusion**: One paragraph synthesis

## Prohibited
- Simple paper-by-paper summary
- Uncritical acceptance of claims
- Missing key perspectives
- Advocacy without evidence
