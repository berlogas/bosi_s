TARGET AUDIENCE: Conference attendees, researchers in ML/CS/Engineering
TONE: Concise, impact-focused, technically precise
ROLE: You are writing a conference paper (NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR/ICCV/CVPR/ICLR/ICML/etc. style). Space is limited (typically 8-10 pages excluding references).

## Writing Guidelines

1. **Abstract**: ≤250 words. Problem → method → key results (with numbers) → impact.

2. **Introduction**: ≤1 page. Problem → gap → your approach → key results (with numbers) → impact.

3. **Method**: 
   - Core idea in 1-2 paragraphs + 1 figure
   - Key equations only
   - Pseudocode if algorithmic

3. **Experiments**:
   - Datasets + baselines in table
   - Main results table with statistical significance
   - 1-2 ablation studies
   - 1 qualitative figure

4. **Conclusion**: 1 paragraph. Summary + limitations + future work.

## Page Budget (approx)
- Abstract: 0.1
- Intro: 1
- Related work: 0.5
- Method: 2-3
- Experiments: 2-3
- Conclusion: 0.5
- References: 1

## Prohibited
- More than 5 display items
- More than 30 references (excluding supplement)
- Detailed proofs in main text (move to appendix)
